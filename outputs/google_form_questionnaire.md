# Questions in the supplied response export

This reference is based on the column headings in `Dma lab  - Form responses 1.csv`. The export contains these survey questions:

1. What is your current year of study?
2. Which broad branch/group are you in?
3. CGPA range
4. How confident are you in your job-related technical skills? (1–5)
5. How regularly are you preparing for placements? (1–5)
6. Have you completed an internship or substantial industry project?
7. How confident are you that you can secure a suitable placement? (1–5)
8. What salary range do you currently expect for your first job?

The exported file does not contain a question about students’ top support priority. The app therefore cannot calculate that result from these responses; it displays salary-range counts instead. The data describes student self-reports and does not include actual job-offer outcomes.
